How decks work

A deck is 8 cards plus 1 tower troop (the tower troop is not one of the 8). Four of the eight slots are special slots; a special card only gets its special behaviour when it sits in a slot that allows it.

Per-deck ceilings

  • Max 2 champions — champion slot + wild slot.
  • Max 2 heroes — hero slot + wild slot. A hero and a champion can coexist (1 each).
  • Max 2 evolutions — evolution slot + wild slot.
  • Special cards total ≤ 4; anything beyond that sits in a normal slot and behaves like the plain card.
  • Every card in the deck must be distinct, and you must own it.

Unlocks

  • Evolution: 6 Evolution Shards unlocks a card's evolved form; evolution levels run 1–3. Shards come from Pass Royale, challenges, events, shop, Lucky Chests, Trophy Road, Evolution Boxes.
  • Hero: 200 Hero Shards unlocks a hero; heroes upgrade with the base card's rarity wild cards. Since 4 Aug 2026 every hero ability is single-use.
  • Evolved form activation: an evolution card must be played a number of times before its evolved form is released; the cycle repeats after each use.
  • Hero ability: cast from above the hand, costs elixir on top of the card's own cost, and lasts only while the hero is alive. The deck's average elixir counts only the base cost.
  • Tower troop: exactly one equipped (Tower Princess, Cannoneer, Dagger Duchess, Royal Chef). It is not part of the 8 cards and levels with its own rarity's wild cards.

Card roles used for suggestions

Every card is classified as win-condition, support, tank, swarm, air-defense, building, spell, cycle, splash, control, air-swarms, tank-killer or utility, with tags for air/ground attack, splash, targeting, cycle cost. A legal ladder deck needs a win path, at least one spell, and two air answers; cycle decks keep average elixir under 3.5.

Hero cards and the API

The developer API does not expose hero cards — no hero rarity, no hero names. Hero availability here is modelled from the 16 released hero forms, matched to the base card you own. Hero unlock state is not readable from the API, so the UI shows hero-capable base cards and marks hero unlock as unknown.
